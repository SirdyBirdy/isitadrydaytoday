Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This policy explains what information isitadrydaytoday.lol ("the site", "we") collects when you use it, and how that information is used.

Location data

When you allow location access, your browser's geolocation coordinates are sent to a reverse-geocoding service (OpenStreetMap Nominatim) to determine your city, state, and country. This is used only to look up the dry day status for your area and is not stored on our servers. If you decline location access, you can type in a city name instead, which is looked up the same way.

Crowdsourced reports

If you submit a "confirm" or "report" button click, your approximate location key (country/state, not precise coordinates) and the date are recorded so other visitors can see aggregate community reports. No personal identifiers are attached to these submissions.

Cookies and advertising

This site displays advertising served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits, in line with the Google Advertising Partners policy.

Local storage

Your browser's local storage is used to remember your theme preference (light/dark) and to prevent duplicate crowdsource submissions on the same day. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Third-party services used by this site

  • Google AdSense — advertising
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim — reverse geocoding / city search
  • Google Fonts — web font delivery

Your choices

You can deny location access at any time through your browser settings, clear local storage through your browser, and opt out of personalized ads through Google's Ads Settings linked above.
